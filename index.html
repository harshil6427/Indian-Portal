India

India is a diverse and vibrant country located in South Asia. It is known for its rich culture, traditions, and history. India is the world's largest democracy and has a population of over 1.4 billion people. The country is famous for landmarks such as the Taj Mahal and its many festivals. India has made significant progress in science, technology, and space exploration. Agriculture and industry both play important roles in its economy. People in India speak many different languages and follow various religions. India's unity in diversity makes it a unique and fascinating nation.
